課文 Bài đọc

現代人的生活已經離不開網路了，生活裡有很多事，比方說買東西、查資料、下載音樂，只要上網，就能做到。住在不同國家的人，也能天天用網路聊天。要是哪裡發生了事情，這些消息，都能透過網路，馬上傳到世界每一個地方，讓大家知道第一手資料。網路替我們節省了時間，還把世界變小了。

雖然網路讓我們的生活變得更便利，但是也有一些問題。很多人每天早上才起床，就急著上網。每五分鐘就要檢查一次郵件，一有新消息、新照片就立刻上傳。他們什麼時候都在上網，好像只有睡覺的時候，才離開網路。在網路上花太多時間，讓很多人的生活變得不正常了。

網路太方便了，讓大家已經習慣透過網路跟朋友打招呼、聊天、談事情，但是不想跟人面對面地說話，人跟人的關係好像越來越遠了。我們應該想一想，怎麼好好地利用網路，才不會影響人跟人的關係。

Bản dịch tiếng Anh / English Translation

Modern day people cannot do without the internet. There are many things in life, for example, buying things, looking up information, and downloading music, that we are able to do if we go online. People living in different countries can talk to each other every day using the internet. If something happens anywhere, the news can be sent immediately everywhere in the world through the internet, allowing everyone to know the information first hand. The internet saves us time and has made the world smaller.

Although the internet makes our lives more convenient, there are some problems. A lot of people are in a hurry to get online first thing after they get up every morning, want to check their email once every five minutes, and as soon as they have the latest news and new photos, they upload/post them online right away. They are online all the time. It seems like only when they are asleep do they get off the internet. Spending too much time online has made a lot of people's lives abnormal.

The internet is too convenient. People have become used to greeting, chatting with, and discussing things with their friends online, but they don't want to talk face-to-face and relationships between people seem to be getting further and further apart. We should think about how we can make good use of the internet so that it doesn't influence relationships between people.

語法 Ngữ pháp (Grammar)
I Completion of Action with V + 過 guo
Function: The 過 in this lesson follows an action verb, indicating that said action is complete; it is different from the experiential 過 of L3. / Quá khứ trong bài này theo sau động từ hành động, biểu thị hành động đã hoàn thành; khác với 過 trải nghiệm ở Bài 3.
Structures: V + 過 | Cấu trúc: Động từ + 過
Affirmative / Khẳng định
AFF
你們吃過餃子再吃菜。
Eat the dishes after you have done with the dumplings. / Các bạn ăn xong sủi cảo rồi hãy ăn món khác.
AFF
大家今天都跑過一千公尺了。
Everyone ran 1000 meters today (as expected). / Hôm nay mọi ngườ đã chạy một ngàn mét rồi.
AFF
垃圾車剛剛來過了。
The garbage truck has already been here (for the day). / Xe rác vừa mớ đến rồi.
Negation / Phủ định
NEG
他們還沒吃過午飯。
They haven't eaten lunch yet. / Họ chưa ăn trưa.
NEG
這個月他還沒付過房租。
He hasn't paid this month's rent. / Tháng này anh ấy chưa trả tiền nhà.
NEG
今天我還沒練習過太極拳。
I haven't practiced Tai Chi today. / Hôm nay tôi chưa tập Thái Cực Quyền.
Questions / Câu hỏi
Q
你喝過咖啡了嗎？
Have you had coffee already? / Bạn uống cà phê chưa?
Q
今年大家都回過國了嗎？
Has everyone gone back to their home country this year? / Năm nay mọi ngườ đã về nước chưa?
Q
那個電影你已經看過了嗎？
Have you already seen that movie? / Bạn đã xem bộ phim đó chưa?
Usage / Cách dùng
USAGE
我做完功課了。 / 我做好功課了。 / 我做過功課了。
The first two sentences (V 完 and V 好) simply state the fact that homework has been completed. The third sentence (V 過) has added implication, and is often used in response to suggestions like "shouldn't you do your homework?" or "go do your homework".
USAGE
A: 要不要喝咖啡？ B: 我剛剛喝過（咖啡）了。
A: Would you like some coffee? B: (No,) I just had some.
USAGE
A: 快考試了，趕快去看書。 B: 我已經看過（書）了。
A: There is a test coming up, you should be studying. B: I have already studied (and I am ready for the test).
USAGE
A: 等一下我們去跑步吧！ B: 我們不是昨天已經跑過了嗎？
A: Let's go for a run! B: Didn't we already go running yesterday?

II Verb Particle 走 zou (away)
Function: 走, when serving as a verb particle, usually indicates a movement of the object away from the speaker due to the subject's action. / 走 khi làm trợ từ động từ, thường biểu thị vật chuyển động ra xa ngườ nói do hành động của chủ thể.
Structures: Verb + 走 | Cấu trúc: Động từ + 走
Affirmative / Khẳng định
AFF
我們都把客人送走了。
We sent off all the guests. / Chúng tôi đã tiễn hết khách.
AFF
請你把門口的那些東西推走。
Please push away all those things from the door. / Làm ơn đẩy những thứ ở cửa đi.
AFF
那件衣服已經被別人買走了。
That dress has already been purchased (and taken away) by someone. / Cái áo đó đã bị ngườ khác mua mất.
Negation / Phủ định
NEG
那幾本書我沒借走，還在圖書館裡。
I did not take out those books; they are still in the library. / Mấy quyển sách đó tôi không mượn, còn ở thư viện.
NEG
他沒騎走你的腳踏車，你的車還停在家裡呢！
He didn't ride away on your bike. Your bike is still parked at home. / Anh ấy không chạy xe đạp của bạn đi, xe bạn còn để ở nhà!
NEG
你不搬走門口這張桌子，我的家具搬不進去。
If you don't move this desk away from the entrance, I can't move my furniture in. / Bạn không chuyển cái bàn ở cửa đi, đồ nội thất của tôi không chuyển vào được.
Questions / Câu hỏi
Q
昨天的作業，老師收走了沒有？
Has the teacher picked up yesterday's homework? / Bài tập hôm qua, thầy giáo thu đi chưa?
Q
偷手機的那個人是不是跑走了？
Did the guy who stole the cell phone run off? / Ngườ ăn trộm điện thoại có phải chạy mất rồi không?
Q
銀行的錢都被提走了嗎？
Has all the bank's money been withdrawn? / Tiền ngân hàng đã bị rút hết chưa?
Usage / Cách dùng
USAGE 1
把 or 被 constructions are often used with the verb particle 走
(1) 他把我的腳踏車騎走了。He rode away on my bike.
(2) 朋友把我的書帶走了。A friend took my book away.
(3) 我的手機被誰拿走了？Who took my cell phone away?
USAGE 2
The following verbs combine with 走：拿'take', 搬'move', 推'push', 騎'ride', 借'borrow', 買'buy', 帶'bring', 約'have date/appointment with (someone)', 選'select, choose', 送'send', 偷'steal', 提'to withdraw (as of money)' and 罵'scold'.
The object can be a concrete thing, but can also be something abstract, e.g., 他離開的時候把快樂的氣氛也帶走了。When he left, he took away with him the pleasant ambiance.
USAGE 3
得／不 can be inserted for the potential form of the pattern.
(1) 這麼多書，你拿得走嗎？This many books!? Are you able to take them all away?
(2) 他告訴大家他決定留在這裡，誰也罵不走他。He told everyone that he has decided to stay, so no matter who tells him off it's no use.
(3) 家具太多了，你可能一次搬不走。There is too much furniture. You probably won't be able to move it away all at once.

A: 你把背包放在椅子上，沒關係嗎？ B: 放心，只要關著門，誰也______。
III Imperative Sentences with 叫 jiào
Function: 叫 here is a transitive verb to indicate that someone asks or makes someone else do something or get something done. / 叫 ở đây là động từ ngoại động từ biểu thị ai đó yêu cầu hoặc bắt ngườ khác làm gì.
Structures: Subject + 叫 + Object + VP | Cấu trúc: Chủ ngữ + 叫 + Tân ngữ + Động từ
Examples / Ví dụ
AFF
媽媽叫我先把包水餃的材料準備好。
Mom asked me to first get all the ingredients for wrapping dumplings ready. / Mẹ bảo tôi chuẩn bị sẵn nguyên liệu gói sủi cảo trước.
AFF
房東叫我們把新家具都搬到樓上房間去。
The landlord asked us to move all the new furniture to the room upstairs. / Chủ nhà bảo chúng tôi chuyển đồ nội thất mới lên phòng tầng trên.
AFF
老師叫學生進教室參加考試。
The teacher told the students to go inside the classroom to take the exam. / Thầy giáo bảo học sinh vào phòng thi.
AFF
醫生叫我多休息。
The doctor told me to rest more. / Bác sĩ bảo tôi nghỉ ngơi nhiều.
AFF

When you travel, do you send lots of texts to your girlfriend every day? / Khi bạn đi du lịch, có phải mỗi ngày gửi rất nhiều tin nhắn cho bạn gái?
Usage / Cách dùng
USAGE 1
In daily conversation, 每 is sometimes omitted, but then the time expression is spoken with a stress.
(1) 明華（每）半年跟大學朋友吃一次飯。Minghua dines with his friends from college every six months.
(2) 老師（每）三天就考一次聽寫。The teacher gives a dictation test every three days.
USAGE 2
Verb classifiers, if any, are positioned either next to the verb or after the object. The former position is preferred.
a. 張先生每半個月去一趟越南。/ b. 張先生每半個月去越南一趟。
a. 你每個月上幾次教堂？/ b. 你每個月上教堂幾次？
他每個月送兩次禮物給女朋友。
*他每個月送禮物給女朋友兩次。 (This sentence is not acceptable)
USAGE 3
When 就 is used in this pattern, it indicates that the frequency is higher than expected; if the frequency is lower than expected, 才 can be used.
(1) 張主任每半年請我們吃一次飯。(factual statement)
(2) 張主任每半年就請我們吃一次飯。(he's a generous person)
(3) 張主任每半年才請我們吃一次飯。(he's a stingy person)

IX 只有 zhǐyǒu … 才 cái … (cannot…, unless…)
Function: In this pattern, 只有 introduces a condition, which must be satisfied before the 才 clause can be carried out. / Trong cấu trúc này, 只有 đưa ra điều kiện phải được đáp ứng trước khi mệnh đề 才 có thể thực hiện.
Structures: 只有 + Condition, 才 + Result | Cấu trúc: 只有 + Điều kiện, 才 + Kết quả
Examples / Ví dụ
AFF

AFF

AFF

Usage / Cách dùng
USAGE
'只有…，才…' and '只要…，就…' (L12, point 4) both indicate conditionals. However, in '只有…，才…' the said condition is harder to meet.
只有懂法文，才能到那家公司工作。(few people understand French) / Chỉ có hiểu tiếng Pháp mới làm việc ở công ty đó.
只要懂法文，就能到那家公司工作。(many people understand French) / Chỉ cần hiểu tiếng Pháp, là có thể làm việc ở công ty đó.

Taiwan's "Bowed-head Tribe" / "Bộ tộc cúi đầu" của Đài Loan

Thanks to the proliferation of mobile devices and easy internet access, everywhere you look, you can see people with their heads lowered, staring at their hand-held units. In Western countries these folks are known as "smartphone addicts." In Taiwan, we call them members of the "bowed-head tribe."

Whether taking public transportation, standing in line, walking, eating, going to the bathroom, or even driving their cars, these people are always clutching onto their smartphones. When chatting or eating with others, their eyes never leave their smartphone screens. No matter what they're doing, they always keep one hand free for smartphone use.

What do they use their smartphones for? Playing online games, browsing social networks, sending text messages, watching movies, surfing the internet, etc. None of these activities are urgent, but these people always have at least half of their attention focused on their phones, often trying to do two or more things at once.

Smart phone addiction is an international phenomenon, but the situation seems to be especially serious in Taiwan. According to statistics provided by a well known website, 64% of Taiwan smart phone users are online every day, a higher rate than either that in the US or Great Britain. Survey results have also shown that over 80% of Taiwanese never leave home without their mobile devices, Asia's highest rate of cell phone dependence.

So, what do you think of the "bowed-head tribe?" Are you one of them?
